Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 09 March 2025
Fusion Edge Games

At Fusion Edge Games, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you download and use our games and applications.

By accessing or using our apps, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. However, we use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data to improve gameplay and user experience.

  • Game Progress: Level progression and in-game activity to understand player interaction
  • Device Information: Device type and operating system version
  • In-App Usage: Anonymous interaction data such as time spent and feature usage

No personal data is required to use our games, and the data collected through Firebase Analytics is not linked to any personal identity.

2. How We Use Information

  • Monitor game performance and user progress
  • Analyze feature usage to improve gameplay
  • Identify bugs and optimize stability
  • Enhance overall user experience

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information because we do not collect any personal data. Anonymous and aggregated data may be shared with trusted third-party service providers such as Firebase solely for analytics and performance improvement purposes.

4. Security

Although we do not collect personal information, all analytics data is processed securely in accordance with Firebase’s security and privacy standards. No electronic storage method is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

5. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

Our games may display non-personalized advertisements served by Google AdMob. These ads comply with COPPA requirements and do not collect or share personal information.

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics is used to collect anonymous gameplay statistics. Learn more at: Firebase Privacy Policy

Our games may also include links to third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services.

6. Children's Privacy

Our games are intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Ads shown to children comply with COPPA and are non-personalized. If we become aware of accidental data collection from a child, we will promptly delete it.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Since no personal data is collected, there is no personal information to manage. Users may opt out of Firebase Analytics through their device or app settings where supported.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be posted within the app or on this page. Continued use of our apps indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
